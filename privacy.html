4XClip – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

4XClip is a desktop program that runs on the user's own computer. It creates short vertical clips from live streams and publishes them to the user's own social media accounts, including TikTok, when the user asks it to.

Information we access

When you connect your TikTok account, 4XClip receives from TikTok: your basic profile information (username and display name) and an access token that lets the program publish videos to your account. 4XClip does not read your messages, followers, or any other private data.

How the information is used

The profile name is shown inside the program so you know which account is connected. The access token is used only to upload the videos you approve to your own TikTok account.

Where the information is stored

All information stays on your own computer, in the program's folder. 4XClip has no servers. We do not collect, sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. The videos you publish are sent directly from your computer to TikTok.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect TikTok at any time from the program's settings, which deletes the saved access token. You can also revoke access from the TikTok app under Settings > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions. Deleting the program's folder removes all stored data.

4XClip – Política de privacidad

4XClip es un programa de escritorio que funciona en la computadora del usuario. Crea clips verticales a partir de directos y los publica en las cuentas del propio usuario, incluida TikTok, cuando el usuario lo pide.

Qué datos usa: al vincular TikTok, el programa recibe el nombre de usuario y un permiso (token) para publicar videos en esa cuenta. No lee mensajes, seguidores ni otros datos privados.

Para qué: mostrar qué cuenta está vinculada y subir los videos que el usuario aprueba.

Dónde se guardan: solo en la computadora del usuario. 4XClip no tiene servidores y no comparte ni vende datos.

Cómo borrarlos: desvinculando TikTok en los ajustes del programa, o quitando el permiso desde la app de TikTok.
