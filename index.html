4XClip

4XClip is a desktop tool for streamers and clip creators. It turns live streams into short vertical clips with subtitles and publishes them to the creator's own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts, after the creator reviews and approves each clip.

4XClip runs on the creator's own computer. It has no servers and does not collect personal data.
